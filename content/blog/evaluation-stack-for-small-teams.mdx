Small teams often skip evaluation because the tooling looks heavy. You don't need a platform to start.

1. **A spreadsheet** with one row per test case: input, expected behaviour, notes.
2. **A script** that runs every case through your current prompt and writes the outputs to a new column.
3. **A habit**: nobody merges a prompt change without looking at the diff.

When the spreadsheet hits a few hundred rows, *then* reach for a platform — and you'll know exactly what you need from it.

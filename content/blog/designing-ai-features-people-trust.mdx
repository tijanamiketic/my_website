Trust in an AI feature is mostly a design problem. Three patterns do most of the work:

## Show your sources

If an answer comes from documents, link them. Users forgive mistakes they can check.

## Admit uncertainty

"I'm not sure — here's what I found" beats a confident wrong answer every time.

## Fail gracefully

Design the empty, error and "I can't help with that" states with the same care as the happy path. They are where trust is won or lost.

The biggest unlock in my workshops isn't a prompt template. It's a mental model: **treat the model like a brilliant new colleague who has never seen your company.**

## Context beats cleverness

People new to AI reach for magic words. What works is context: who the output is for, what good looks like, and what to avoid.

## Show, don't describe

One good example in the prompt does more than a paragraph of adjectives. I ask participants to paste a past email they liked before asking for a new one.

## Iterate out loud

We practise reading the output critically and saying *why* it misses — then putting that reason back into the prompt. After three rounds, most people never go back to one-shot prompting.

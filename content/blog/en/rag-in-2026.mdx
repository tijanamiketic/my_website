Every few months someone declares retrieval-augmented generation dead because context windows got bigger. Context did get bigger — and RAG changed shape rather than disappearing.

## What changed

- **Chunking matters less.** With a million tokens available, you can retrieve whole documents instead of 500-token fragments.
- **Reranking matters more.** The bottleneck moved from "fit it in" to "put the right thing first".

## What didn't

- **Freshness.** Your data still changes after training.
- **Citations.** Users still need to see where an answer came from.
- **Cost.** Sending everything on every request is still expensive.

The best systems I see today retrieve generously, rerank carefully and cite everything.

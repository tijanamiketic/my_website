You've probably wondered by now: of all the LLMs out there, which one should I use? Some people swear by ChatGPT, others by Claude, and a third group thinks both are awful and will only use Gemini. Meanwhile, you can't see any difference between them, so you can't tell what might suit whom.

Below, I break down the differences between a few of the best-known LLMs. The point is simple: none of them is bad — they just often get used the wrong way, for the wrong things.

## The five best-known models and what each one is good for

1. **GPT:** The best everyday assistant, like a smart friend who's always around. It remembers you across conversations, understands your goals and needs, and tailors its answers to you, which makes it great for planning, getting organised and working out a strategy. It also has voice conversations and makes images, so it's the best choice for someone just getting started with AI.
2. **Claude:** At its strongest when serious thinking is needed: planning, strategy, coding and writing documentation. It writes the most naturally of them all and follows complex instructions precisely, so it's excellent for texts, emails and reports that don't sound “robotic”. You can give it long documents or contracts and ask for an analysis or a summary.
3. **Gemini:** The best choice if you already use Gmail, Google Drive and YouTube, because it's connected to them. It makes images with Nano Banana and video with Veo, and it handles video and long files well. It also comes with NotebookLM, Google's tool built on the same model that works on the RAG principle (Retrieval-Augmented Generation — generating answers by searching your sources): you add your documents, links or YouTube videos, and it answers only from them, showing where each piece of information came from. I'll write more about what a RAG system is and how it works in an upcoming post.
4. **Grok:** It follows the X (Twitter) network in real time, so it knows what's happening right now. It's good for quick look-ups and for critiquing your documentation, text or idea, because it's direct and doesn't sugar-coat. For important facts, though, double-check its answer somewhere else.
5. **DeepSeek:** It's free, and very strong at logical reasoning, maths and code, which makes it a good free alternative for more demanding tasks. When you switch on its thinking mode, you can see its whole chain of reasoning — useful if you want to learn from the answers. Important: your data goes to servers in China, so don't give it personal or business information.

## The real question

Once we understand what each of these models is for and how to use it, using them gets simpler and we reach our goals faster. Instead of asking “which LLM is the best?”, the real questions become: what do I actually need, and which tool does that best?

On November 12, Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen and put it into use right away. This isn't a model for the hardest, multi-day tasks. It's the **fast member of the Gemini 4 family**, built for everyday work: coding, agents and ordinary queries. Hydrogen also replaces the old "Flash" line.

## Where it fits in the Gemini 4 family

Google has shipped three models in just six weeks:

- **Argon** (announced September 30, available to everyone from October 13) is for deep reasoning and demanding multi-step tasks.
- **Carbon** (announced October 28) is the most capable model in the family, focused on cybersecurity and long agentic workflows. Since November 10 it has been available to Google AI Ultra subscribers.
- **Hydrogen** (November 12) is fast, broad and meant for everyone.

## The numbers Google cites

- **2.5 times faster** than Gemini 3.8 Flash.
- On **Terminal-Bench 4.1**, which measures how well an agent works in the command line, it scores 57%. Its predecessor scored 18.2%, a more than threefold improvement.
- **More than five times fewer made-up claims** (hallucinations) than its predecessor.
- On the independent Artificial Analysis index it scores 51 points at the "high" setting and 54 at the strongest "xhigh" setting. That puts it level with the larger Argon model (50), and even slightly above it.

## Price and availability

The pricing is simple: **$1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens**, the same across the full one-million-token context. There's also a free tier in the API.

Availability matters even more. Hydrogen has become the **default model for AI Mode in Google Search**. Free users of the Gemini app get it automatically, and developers can use it in Google AI Studio, through the Gemini API and on Vertex AI. Google isn't keeping it behind an expensive subscription; it's treating it as the new workhorse model for everyone.

## The fine print

To complete the picture:

- "Cheap" is relative. Hydrogen is currently **twice as expensive** as Gemini 3.8 Flash, which is on a promotional price until December 31. From January 1, 2027, their prices will be the same.
- Most of the results come from Google's own tests.
- Even in Google's own table, Hydrogen trails Claude Sonnet 5.5 (63.9%) on Terminal-Bench.
- Some users still notice that it's slower than some competing fast models.

## Why this matters

In a month full of big announcements about the most powerful models, Hydrogen is probably the model that the most people will actually notice. Anyone using AI Mode in Search is already working with it, even if they don't know it.

In everyday life, faster answers, a lower price and wide availability usually matter more than one more point on a leaderboard. The lesson for anyone building AI products: a model that is "good enough, fast and everywhere" often beats one that is "the best, but expensive and locked away."
